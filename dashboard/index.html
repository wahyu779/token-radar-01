How a value is built

Ranked value = curve × launch share: a $92.7M curve at 12.8% odds ranks $11.9M, below a $26.1M curve at 61.9% ranking $16.2M. The band is the leave-one-out error: P10 ×0.091, P90 ×11.98.

How a farm is priced

slice = drop × curve × deposit / TVL · net = slice − gas · break-even = gas / slice · EV = odds × slice − gas

The slice is cut from the curve value, never the ranked value — the odds are already in the rank. Verdict: FARM when break-even ≤ odds, MARGINAL when odds < break-even ≤ 1, SKIP when break-even > 1. At $1,000 and 7%: 30 FARM / 45 MARGINAL / 123 SKIP.

The plan's own capital is $50–$250 by TVL — money put to work in the protocol, not spent; only the gas is spent. It is not the deposit, which only sizes the slice.

Assumptions, stated plainly

The snapshot is DefiLlama data as of 2026-11-14, 758 protocols at or above $5M TVL. "No token recorded" means the record carries no gecko_id and no symbol — it is not proof a token does not exist. The drop share is a guess (7% by default, change it above) — no protocol on this board has promised one. The launch odds are stock shares, not forecasts. Nothing here is investment advice.
